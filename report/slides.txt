Problem & Motivation
The axial IMU acceleration nor barometric pressure alone gives full state directly
Goal: reconstruct z (altitude), vz (velocity), ba (accel bias) from asynchronous logged data
Why not just integrate IMU?
Bias drift, vibration will lead to unbounded error without correction
Why not just use baro?
No velocity, susceptible to pressure transients

Stochastic Formulation
Rocket Trajectory = Latent random process observed through noisy sensors
xk+1 = f(xk,uk,Δtk) + wk		wk ~ N(0,Qk)
yk     = h(xk)+vk				vk  ~ N(0,Rk)
The state is given by: x = [z, vz, ba]T
Qk process noise: unmodeled thrust, aero, wind
Rk measurement noise: barometer uncertainty
Kalman = MMSE estimator under linear-Gaussian assumptions
h(z) is nonlinear (ISA pressure model)
This is where the extended portion of the EKF comes in

Process Model
Continuous-time dynamics (linear in state):
ż = vz		v̇ = uz-ba+wv		ḃa = wb
Where:		uz = aIMU - g
i.e., approx. 0 on pad and positive at burnout
Discrete predict at inter-event step Δt
Fk =  	1		Δt		-½ Δt2
				0		1		  -Δt
				0		0		    1
Process is linear, Fk is exact and not approx.

Variable Process Noise
Covariance prediction:
Pk- = Fk  Pk-1+  FkT + Qd(Δt)
Qd(Δt) explicitly scaled by inter-event interval
Why is variable-step EKF required? 
IMU:baro sampling varies widely (1:1 to 21:1) with no shared clock, so Δt is non-uniform
process noise must scale with Δt, meaning larger intervals accumulate proportionally more uncertainty 
A fixed-Q assumption would therefore be invalid

Update & Covariance
Standard innovation and gain:
vk = yk - h(xk-)
KK = Pk- HkT Sk-1
Covariance uses Joseph form:
Pk+ = (I - KKHK) Pk- (I-KKHk)T + KkRkKkT
Rather than the simpler P+ = (I - KH) Pk-, as it preserves symmetry and positive- definiteness under finite-precision arithmetic

Asynchronous Replay 
IMU + baro: merged by timestamp into one sorted event stream (tie-break = IMU first)
At every event: predict step with held uz (zero-order hold)
At baro events only: measurement update
No resampling: variable-step handles non-uniform intervals

Discussion 
Estimator architecture & robustness
Nonlinear pressure EKF with linear process; variable-step Qd(Δt) and Joseph form ensured stability (no covariance issues)
Gating disabled: large outliers (+/-4400 Pa) absorbed without divergence
Performance outcomes
14/16 non-Raven flights within +/-5 m accuracy
All 25 flights return finite, stable final states
